---
title: "Boards"
description: "Create, open, rename, and delete boards, and who may do what."
---

The home page lists every board, the one that changed last first, with the date of its last change and the email of the person who created it.

## Who may do what

Everyone whom Access lets in sees every board, opens it, and edits it.

| Action | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Create a board | Everyone |
| Open and edit a board | Everyone |
| Rename a board | Its creator and admins |
| Delete a board | Its creator and admins |

Admins are the emails in `admins` of alcyone.config.ts.

## Create

Choose **New board**, and give the board a name of up to 100 characters. The board opens at once.

## Rename

On the home page, open the menu of the board and choose **Rename**. On the board itself, choose its name at the top left.
Everyone on the board sees the new name at once.

## Delete

On the home page, open the menu of the board and choose **Delete**.
alcyone closes the board for everyone on it, deletes the board and its images from the bucket, and takes it off the list. A deleted board cannot come back.
