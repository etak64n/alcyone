---
title: "Edit a board"
description: "The tools, the selection, text, lines, images, and the keys of a board."
---

The tools are on the left side of a board. The bar at the top shows the colors and the sizes of what you select, or of what the tool in use makes.

## Tools

| Tool | Key | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Select | V | Selects, moves, and resizes items. |
| Hand | H | Moves the board. |
| Sticky note | N | Places a note where you click, and starts its text. |
| Text | T | Places a text box where you click, and starts its text. |
| Rectangle | R | Draws a rectangle by a drag, or places one with a click. |
| Ellipse | O | Draws an ellipse. |
| Diamond | D | Draws a diamond. |
| Line | L | Draws a line by a drag. A line that starts or ends on an item joins it. |
| Pen | P | Draws freehand. The pen stays in use until you choose another tool. |
| Image | | Adds images from your device. |

After it places an item, every tool but the pen goes back to Select.

## Select

- Click an item to select it. Shift-click adds an item to the selection or takes it out.
- Drag on an empty part of the board to select every item that the rectangle touches.
- Cmd+A (Ctrl+A) selects every item. Escape selects nothing.

Drag a selected item to move the selection. The arrow keys move it by 1, or by 10 with Shift.

A single selected item shows a handle at each corner. Sticky notes and images keep their proportions when you resize them, unless you hold Shift. A text box takes the width of the drag, and its text decides its height.

## Text

Double-click a note, a text box, or a shape, or press Enter on a selected one, to write in it. Escape, Cmd+Enter (Ctrl+Enter), or a click outside ends the text.
A sticky note and a shape make their text smaller as it grows, so that it fits. A text box grows downward with its text, and an empty text box goes away when you finish.

## Lines

A line that you draw from an item to another item joins them: its ends sit on the outlines of the items and follow them when they move. To join an end to another item, select the line and drag the end onto the item. An end that you drop on the empty board stays where you leave it.

When you delete an item that a line joins, the end of the line stays where it was.

## Images

Add an image with the Image tool, by a drop on the board, or by a paste. alcyone takes PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, and AVIF images of up to 20 MB, and keeps them in the bucket of the board.

## Style and arrange

The bar at the top changes what you select, and what the tool makes next:

- the color of a sticky note
- the color of text, of the outline of a shape, of a line, and of a pen stroke
- the fill of a shape
- the size of a text box, from S to XL
- the arrowheads of a line
- the width of a pen stroke

With a selection, the bar also brings it to the front, sends it to the back, duplicates it, and deletes it.

## Copy and paste

Cmd+C (Ctrl+C) copies the selection, and Cmd+V (Ctrl+V) pastes it around the pointer, on the same board or another one. Lines between copied items join the copies. Plain text that you paste becomes a text box.

## Undo

Cmd+Z (Ctrl+Z) undoes your last edit, and Shift+Cmd+Z (Ctrl+Y) redoes it. Undo takes back your own edits only. It puts back what you changed even when someone changed the board after you.

## Move around

| To | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Move the board | Scroll, drag with the Hand tool, drag while you hold Space, or drag with the middle button. On a touch screen, drag with one finger on the empty board. |
| Zoom | Cmd (Ctrl) and scroll, or pinch. The buttons at the bottom right zoom too. |
| Zoom to 100% | Cmd+0 (Ctrl+0) |
| See every item | Shift+1 |

Each board opens where you last left it in this browser.
